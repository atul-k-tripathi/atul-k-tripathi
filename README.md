## 🔬 Research

### 01 · Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems

**Published Research · Springer · 2026**

A comparative study of multiple knowledge-representation paradigms for healthcare question answering using progressively complex clinical queries.

- 47 anonymized cardiovascular patient records
- 5 benchmark clinical queries
- Evaluation across query coverage, reasoning depth and explainability
- Comparative implementations involving Python, SQLite, JSON, RDF/OWL, RDFLib, OWL-RL and SPARQL
- Knowledge Graphs achieved complete benchmark query coverage with multi-hop and contextual reasoning

**Publication:**  
Tripathi, A. K., Thakre, P. M., & Chatterjee, N.  
*Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems: A Comparative Analysis of Performance of Different Representation Paradigms.*  
Springer, 2026.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-24807-7_4)

### 02 · Cryptocurrency Markets and Geopolitical Risk

**Ongoing Research**

Collaborative high-frequency research examining cryptocurrency and traditional financial markets under geopolitical shocks.

Current methodological work includes:

- Construction of an Hourly War Risk Index from time-stamped geopolitical news
- Event identification and alignment with hourly market data
- Abnormal-return analysis using AR, CAR and CAAR
- Multiple event windows ranging from 1–48 hours
- Statistical testing and multiple-testing control

This project is currently under research and review and is not presented here as a completed publication.
